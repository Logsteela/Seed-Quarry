## Seed Quarry modifications

Seed Quarry adds and modifies features including searchable structure
variants, Java 1.16 structure-Loot conditions, chest-coordinate rules,
experimental village and bastion layout analysis, optional 48-bit Loot search
optimisations, dialog and settings changes, localisation support, development
tools, and Windows packaging.

These descriptions identify the scope of the fork's modifications; they do
not imply authorship of the underlying viewer or generation engines.
